❓Choosing a class

Questions about classes

Which class should my child start in?+

Start with your child’s school class. In the first week a short, playful check-in adjusts the level, and you can switch classes at any time.

Can siblings in different classes share one account?+

Yes. The Family plan covers up to three children, each with their own class, progress and badges.

What if my child finishes a class early?+

They can unlock bonus missions or move up to the next class whenever their mentor agrees they are ready.

Do lessons follow the school curriculum?+

Every class is mapped to primary school standards for reading, maths and science, with extra time for art, coding and life skills.
