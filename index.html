❓Questions

Things parents often ask

Still curious?Our family team replies within one school day.
Which class should my child start in?+

Start with your child’s school class. A short, playful check-in during the first week fine-tunes the level, and you can switch classes any time.

How much screen time is involved?+

Lessons are 15–35 minutes depending on the class, and most of that time is spent building, drawing or experimenting away from the screen.

Do we need special materials?+

No. Activities use everyday things like paper, blocks, crayons and kitchen items. Printable packs are included in the Family plan.

Is Brightbud safe for my child?+

Yes. There are no ads, no public chats and no contact with strangers. All mentors are trained educators with background checks.

Can teachers use Brightbud in class?+

Absolutely. The School plan includes class dashboards and curriculum mapping, and teachers can try it free.
