Getting started

Setting up your account and first lesson.

How do I sign up?+

Tap “Start free”, add your email and your child’s class, and your first lesson is ready in under two minutes.

Which class should my child join?+

Start with their school class. A playful check-in in week one fine-tunes the level, and you can switch any time.

What devices work?+

Any tablet, laptop or phone with a modern web browser. Our parent app is on iOS and Android.

Learning

Lessons, clubs and progress.

How long is a lesson?+

15 to 35 minutes depending on the class, and most of that time is spent building, drawing or experimenting.

Do we need special materials?+

No. Activities use paper, blocks, crayons and kitchen items you already have.

How do I see my child’s progress?+

The free parent app shows a weekly report, badges earned and notes from their mentor.

Safety

How we keep children safe.

Is Brightbud safe for children?+

Yes. No ads, no public chats and no contact with strangers. Read more on our Safety page.

Who are the mentors?+

Qualified primary teachers who pass enhanced background checks and child-safety training.

What data do you collect?+

Only what we need to run lessons: your email, your child’s first name and their progress. We never sell data.

Billing

Plans, trials and refunds.

What happens after the free trial?+

We remind you two days before it ends. If you do nothing, your account pauses. You’re never charged without choosing a plan.

Can I cancel any time?+

Yes, with one tap in the parent app. No phone calls, no forms.

Is there a refund policy?+

Contact us within 30 days of paying for a full refund.
