🧭How mentoring works

A grown-up in their corner

From the first hello to the end-of-year celebration, your child’s mentor guides every step.

1

Meet & match

A friendly first call to learn your child’s interests and how they like to learn.

2

Set little goals

Together they choose 2–3 goals for the term, like reading a whole book.

3

Weekly check-ins

A 10-minute chat each week to celebrate wins and tackle tricky bits.

4

Celebrate

Termly reports for parents and a badge ceremony for every goal reached.

❓Safety & mentors

Parents often ask

How are mentors chosen?+

Every mentor holds a teaching qualification, passes an enhanced background check and completes our child-safety and wellbeing training before meeting any children.

Will my child have the same mentor all year?+

Yes. Children keep one main mentor for the whole school year, so they build real trust.

Can I talk to my child’s mentor?+

Of course. Message them any time in the parent app and book a video chat once a month.

Are sessions recorded?+

Group sessions are monitored for safety. Recordings are stored securely and only shared with parents on request.
