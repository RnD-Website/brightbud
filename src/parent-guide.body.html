🚀Your first week

Getting started in 4 easy steps

Most families are fully settled in by the end of week one.

Step 1 · Day 1

Download the parent app

Turn on weekly reports and lesson reminders so you never miss a win.

Step 2 · Day 1

Set up a learning corner

A quiet table, good light and a box of crayons, paper and blocks is all you need.

Step 3 · Day 2

Meet the mentor

Join the short welcome call so your child’s mentor can learn what they love.

Step 4 · Day 3

Pick a daily time

Same time, same place. Short and regular beats long and rare.

💡Everyday tips

Little habits, big results

Praise the effort

Say “You worked so hard on that!” rather than “You’re so clever.”

Ask them to teach you

Explaining a lesson back is one of the best ways to remember it.

Screens off before bed

Finish lessons at least an hour before bedtime for better sleep.
