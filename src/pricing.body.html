Pricing questions

What happens after the free trial?+

We remind you two days before it ends. If you do nothing, your account simply pauses. You are never charged without choosing a plan.

Can I switch plans later?+

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or switch between monthly and yearly at any time from the parent app.

Do you offer sibling or hardship discounts?+

The Family plan already covers up to three children. Get in touch if cost is a barrier and we will do our best to help.

Is there a refund policy?+

If Brightbud is not right for your family, contact us within 30 days of paying for a full refund.
