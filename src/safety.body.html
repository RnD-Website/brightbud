🔒Three layers of protection

Safe by design

We combine trusted people, a closed platform and careful data handling.

Trusted people

Every adult your child meets is vetted.

  • Qualified primary teachers
  • Enhanced background checks
  • Yearly safeguarding training

Closed platform

A walled garden built only for kids.

  • No ads or outside links
  • No private chat between children
  • Live sessions always monitored

Careful with data

We collect as little as possible.

  • First names only for children
  • Data encrypted and never sold
  • Delete your account any time
